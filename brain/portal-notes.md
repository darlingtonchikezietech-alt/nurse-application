### 2026-11-12 — setup
- Public sources point to `licence.nmcn.gov.ng` for renewal and change of name, `portal.nmcn.gov.ng` for the Professional Update Form. Renewal requires an approved PUF.
- Portal FAQ: an RRR generated outside the portal means completing the application on paper at an NMCN office, so always generate the RRR inside the portal.
- Diaspora nurses need WCEA CPD credits (3 credit units) at renewal time.
